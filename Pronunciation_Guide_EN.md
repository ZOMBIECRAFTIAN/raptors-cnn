# Pronunciation guide for tricky terms

This file lists the scientific names, technical terms, and phrases in the presentation that are easy to mispronounce when speaking English as a non-native.

Each row shows:

- **The written word**
- **A phonetic approximation in plain Spanish-friendly spelling** (read it as Spanish)
- **A short note** if the stress pattern matters

Practice each row out loud twice.

## Numbers — say them in full

Native speakers say numbers in groups of two when possible:

- 53 = "fifty-three" (not "five-three")
- 2024 = "twenty twenty-four" (not "two thousand twenty-four")
- 0.85 = "zero point eight five" (not "zero comma eighty-five")
- 1e-4 = "one times ten to the minus four"
- 95% = "ninety-five percent"
- 70 / 15 / 15 = "seventy, fifteen, fifteen"
- 4.0 / 5 = "four out of five"
